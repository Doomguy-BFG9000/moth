# Bob

Bob is a lackadaisical teenager. Write `response`, which takes what was said to him and gives back his answer:

- `Sure.` to a question (it ends with a question mark, spaces after it aside);
- `Whoa, chill out!` to yelling (it has letters, and they're all capitals);
- `Calm down, I know what I'm doing!` to a yelled question;
- `Fine. Be that way!` to silence (nothing, or only spaces, tabs and new lines);
- `Whatever.` to anything else.
